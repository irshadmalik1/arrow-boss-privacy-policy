1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Boss ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our mobile application Arrow Boss: Tap Away Puzzle (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you download, install, and play Arrow Boss, how such information is utilized, and your choices regarding your privacy rights.

2. Information We Collect

Arrow Boss is designed to prioritize user privacy and offline functionality. We do not require you to create an account, log in with social networks, or provide personal identifiable credentials to play.

A. Gameplay & Progress Data (Stored Locally)

Your game levels, coins, gems, win streaks, unlocked color themes, and custom player name are stored locally on your device. We use secure on-device preferences with anti-tamper mechanisms to protect your save file without sending personal game states to external servers.

B. Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App with an active internet connection (e.g., to watch rewarded ads, check remote updates, or play 1v1 online matches), standard technical information may be collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs:

  • Device attributes (such as device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and system language)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), subject to your device settings and consent
  • Anonymous diagnostics, network connectivity status, and crash reports to maintain application stability

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App integrates reputable third-party services provided by Google to support monetization, in-app purchases, and functionality. These providers operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core application maintenance, security verification, and platform integrity.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK): Used to deliver optional banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads (such as earning coins, revives, or gems). Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to deliver relevant ads or non-personalized ads based on your age and consent settings.
    How Google Uses Information from Partner Apps
  • Google Play In-App Billing (Billing Library 8.0.0): Used to securely process purchases of "Arrow Boss Premium" and optional virtual items. All financial transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play; Arrow Boss never has access to your credit card or financial billing information.
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config & Cloud Messaging): Used for anonymous remote configuration (e.g., tuning level balancing, events) and push notifications if enabled by the user.
    Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its intended gameplay experience:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to access online 1v1 AI duels, load rewarded video ads, verify in-app purchases, and check remote game config.
  • VIBRATE: Required to provide tactile haptic feedback during arrow taps, collision bounces, and puzzle completions. Can be toggled off at any time in Settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission on Android 13+ to notify you about daily rewards or special puzzle challenges. Can be enabled or disabled in Settings.
Zero Sensitive Permissions: Arrow Boss does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, or external storage files.

5. Children's Privacy & Age Gate

Arrow Boss is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements:

  • The App includes an initial Age Gate dialog.
  • For users identified as under the age of 13, interest-based behavioral advertising is strictly disabled, and all ad requests are flagged for child-directed treatment (TFCD/TFUA compliance).
  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • In-App Data Erasure: You can completely wipe all locally stored game data, coin balances, level progression, and settings at any time by navigating to Settings → Reset Progress.
  • Ad Tracking Choices: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads. Users in the EEA/UK can also update their privacy consent preferences anytime via the in-app Privacy & Ad Consent (UMP) menu.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics/Ads: You can play Arrow Boss in full offline mode without an active network connection.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices, including cryptographic checks, memory obfuscation, and secure HTTPS communications, to safeguard all data interactions within the application.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly within this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.
